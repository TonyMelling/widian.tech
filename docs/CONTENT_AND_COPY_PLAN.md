# Content and Copy Plan

## Editorial objective

Make Widian's category and value understandable before describing features: one structured building record connects every accountable party and preserves the evidence trail from inspection to verified closure.

## Message order

1. **Category:** the shared, verified operational building record.
2. **Outcome:** know what is true, know what needs action, prove what happened.
3. **Mechanism:** controlled lifecycle, accountable roles and evidence-backed transitions.
4. **Differentiation:** operational coordination rather than disconnected reports or passive storage.
5. **Proof:** only verified product behaviour, demonstrations and approved customer evidence.
6. **Action:** book a demo.

## Copy rules

- Use British English, short sentences and concrete verbs.
- Use “recorded”, “submitted”, “reviewed”, “remediated”, “independently checked”, “client verified” and “closed” precisely.
- Say who acts, what evidence is added and what permits a status change.
- Never claim Widian certifies statutory compliance or replaces professional judgement.
- Avoid “revolutionary” as an unsupported self-description. Demonstrate the market change through the operating model.
- Do not use invented statistics, customers, awards, accreditations or testimonials.
- Label proposed and future capabilities visibly.

## Content workflow

1. Copywriter drafts against verified product evidence and page intent.
2. Product owner checks functional accuracy.
3. Fire-safety reviewer checks terminology and professional implications.
4. Legal/privacy owner reviews regulated, security and data-use statements.
5. Creative director edits hierarchy and voice without weakening qualifications.
6. Content designer tests scanability at 320 px and in page context.
7. SEO reviewer confirms search intent, unique metadata, internal links and content distinctiveness without weakening the narrative.

## Required source material

- Founder/category narrative and origin of Widian
- Confirmed product capability matrix with screenshots or demo access
- Definitions of independent inspection, client verification and closure
- Target customer operating models and procurement concerns
- Approved security, hosting, privacy, resilience and support facts
- Team biographies and company facts
- Real pilot/case-study evidence when permission exists
- Walkthrough ownership, response expectation and qualification questions

## Completion standard

Each page must have a different job, information rhythm and visual story. It must answer its visitor question independently while reinforcing the same category proposition and using only supportable claims.
