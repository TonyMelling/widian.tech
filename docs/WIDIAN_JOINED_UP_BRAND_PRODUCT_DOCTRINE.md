# Widian Joined-up Brand & Product Doctrine

**Status:** Governing website/product alignment context

## Principle

Widian is one brand and one product story expressed through different surfaces.

- `widian.tech` explains, demonstrates and converts.
- `app.widian.tech` performs the operational work.
- reports, evidence packs, email, support and future physical identifiers extend the same system of trust.

The website must not invent a parallel version of Widian. The application must not drift into a separate visual or language system.

## Shared North Star

Widian is the operational infrastructure for trusted, continuous building-compliance records, initially focused on passive fire.

The persistent organising object is the **Building Record**.

Core proposition:

> **One building record. Every responsible party. Verified from inspection to closure.**

Supporting promise:

> **Know what's true. Know what needs action. Prove what happened.**

Circular Compliance extends this with continuity:

> **Know → Inspect → Act → Verify → Prove → Monitor → repeat**

Individual findings/Compliance Cases can close. The Building Record persists.

## Shared Concepts

### Building Record
The authoritative operational record Widian can substantiate for a building, plus the history explaining how its state was reached.

### Golden Thread
The evidence/provenance discipline: what happened, who acted, when, why, what evidence supports it and what changed. It is not empty regulatory marketing language.

### Circular Compliance
The continuous lifecycle model around the persistent Building Record. It is not itself a legal compliance status or certification.

### Widian Thread
The restrained visual/experience metaphor for continuity of identity, evidence, responsibility and verification across website, product, reports and future physical/digital touchpoints.

## Shared Experience

Both website and product should express **quiet technical authority**:

- calm;
- precise;
- evidence-led;
- premium but restrained;
- accessible;
- technically advanced without gimmicks;
- unmistakably Widian.

Use approved typography, logo assets, colour tokens and terminology consistently. Marketing may use more editorial composition and motion; operational software must prioritise task clarity and field usability.

## Product Truth Contract

The website's product claims are downstream of product truth.

Before publishing a current-capability claim:

1. identify the corresponding application capability;
2. verify it against deployed/current authoritative evidence;
3. record the claim in `CLAIMS_REGISTER.md`;
4. qualify limitations that materially change the buyer's understanding;
5. label future/roadmap concepts as future.

Marketing copy never makes a planned feature real.

## Shared Claim Boundaries

Never imply that:

- Widian certifies statutory compliance;
- contractor completion automatically equals closure;
- AI establishes compliance;
- every record is legally immutable/tamper-proof;
- a certification, penetration test, customer, award or network exists before evidence/approval exists.

## Cross-repository Change Rule

A material change to positioning, product terminology, lifecycle semantics, brand tokens, logo use, public capability claims, domain/authentication relationships or Circular Compliance doctrine must trigger a cross-repository impact check.

The change may originate in either repository, but both repositories should be assessed before the work is considered complete.

Do not automatically duplicate implementation-specific documentation between repositories. Synchronise the shared contract, not every internal detail.
