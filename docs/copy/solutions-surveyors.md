## 1. Page objective

Show surveyors that field capture is fast, structured and doesn't depend on a signal.

## 2. Primary audience

Surveyors and inspectors carrying out on-site inspections.

## 3. Visitor question

Can I capture structured evidence efficiently on site, including offline?

## 4. Primary headline

> **Capture findings on site — even without signal.**

*(Proposed — needs Brand Strategist + Senior Copywriter approval.)*

## 5. Short standfirst

> Identify the element, record the finding, add the photo. Minimal typing, and it saves whether or not you have a connection.

## 6. Narrative sequence

Story per `SPEC.md` §7.4: identify the correct element, structured field checks, photographs and findings, minimal typing, offline-first saving, synchronisation visibility.

- **Identify the correct element:** Scan a QR code to bring up the exact element on site — not a search through a list. *[Claim: QR scanning, photo evidence and P1–P5 priorities — Verified.]*
- **Structured field checks:** Findings are captured against a defined structure, not free text alone.
- **Minimal typing:** Priority, status and structured fields do most of the work; typing is for what genuinely needs it.
- **Offline-first saving:** Inspections and findings save to the device first and sync when a connection is available — the core capture-and-sync loop works without signal. *[Claim: Offline inspection capture and sync — Qualified: core loop only. Do not claim full offline capability — scheduling/agenda and full history sync are not offline.]*
- **Synchronisation visibility:** See what's synced and what's still pending, rather than wondering if your work saved.
