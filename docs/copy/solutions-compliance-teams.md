**Status:** Draft for review — not approved. Part of the Solutions role-page set; see `docs/copy/solutions.md`.

## 1. Page objective

Show compliance leaders and fire-safety managers that they can prioritise, own and evidence their team's work without chasing status by phone or spreadsheet.

## 2. Primary audience

Heads of compliance, fire-safety managers, compliance and assurance teams.

## 3. Visitor question

What remains open, overdue, unsupported or unverified?

## 4. Primary headline

> **Know what's overdue, unverified, and whose action it is.**

*(Proposed — needs Brand Strategist + Senior Copywriter approval.)*

## 5. Short standfirst

> Every finding has a priority, an owner and a status. Nothing sits quietly unresolved because no one's checked.

## 6. Narrative sequence

Story per `SPEC.md` §7.4: prioritisation, overdue/unverified actions, QA and evidence review, clear ownership, traceable decisions.

- **Prioritisation:** Findings carry a P1–P5 priority, so the highest-consequence issues are never buried under routine ones. *[Claim: QR scanning, photo evidence and P1–P5 priorities — Verified.]*
- **Overdue and unverified visibility:** See what's still open, what's awaiting review, and what's awaiting client acceptance — without asking.
- **QA and evidence review:** Every inspection is reviewed by someone other than the person who submitted it, before it's treated as confirmed. *[Claim: Independent inspection — Qualified: describe as a reviewer other than the original submitter, not a separate "independent inspector" role.]*
- **Clear ownership:** Every open action has a named owner and a status, not an assumed one.
- **Traceable decisions:** Reviews, approvals and rejections are recorded against the record, with who acted and when. *[Claim: Time-stamped attribution and audit history — Verified.]*

## 7. Proof points

| Proof point | Claim area | Status |
|---|---|---|
| P1–P5 priority | QR scanning, photo evidence and P1–P5 priorities | Verified |
| Self-review forbidden on inspection QA | Independent inspection | Qualified |
| Audit trail on reviews/approvals | Time-stamped attribution and audit history | Verified |
| Client verification for priority findings | Client verification and closure | Qualified — P1/P2 only |

## 8. Objections answered

- *"Isn't QA review just another admin step?"* → 6 — it's the mechanism that prevents self-marked completion, which is the entire point of the page.
- *"Can I trust the priority isn't gamed?"* → priority is set at the finding stage, before remediation begins, and stays visible throughout.
