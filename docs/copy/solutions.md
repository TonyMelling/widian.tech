## 1. Page objective

Route each audience into their relevant value story while keeping the same underlying building record visibly fixed underneath — proving the "one record, many roles" claim structurally, not just by saying it.

## 2. Primary audience

Mixed — visitors self-select by role. No single primary audience; the page's job is fast, accurate self-routing.

## 3. Visitor question

What does the shared record change for my role?

## 4. Primary headline

> **One record. A different view for every role.**

*(Proposed — no suggested headline given in `SPEC.md` §7.4. Needs Brand Strategist + Senior Copywriter approval.)*

## 5. Short standfirst

> The building record doesn't change depending on who's looking at it. What you see — and what you're responsible for — does.

## 6. Narrative sequence

**Layout grammar:** one record viewed through different responsibilities — avoid an independent feature-card collection (`SPEC.md` §7.4). **Dominant interaction:** switch perspective (Building Owner / Compliance Team / Surveyor / Contractor) while the underlying record visualisation stays fixed; only the highlighted layer and framing change.

### 6.1 Hero — one record, four vantage points

Headline/standfirst as above, with the role switcher as the dominant visual.

### 6.2 Role entry points

Four short entry cards (not full feature cards — teasers into each role page):

- **Building Owners:** *"See what's true across your portfolio — not just what's been reported."* → `/solutions/building-owners`
- **Compliance Teams:** *"Know what's overdue, unverified, and whose action it is."* → `/solutions/compliance-teams`
- **Surveyors:** *"Capture findings on site, minimal typing, even offline."* → `/solutions/surveyors`
- **Contractors:** *"Know your scope, submit your evidence, know what happens next."* → `/solutions/contractors`

### 6.3 Closing — the record doesn't move

**Body:** Change the perspective and the record stays exactly where it is. That's the point — everyone is working from the same building, the same findings, the same evidence.
