**Status:** Complete draft for accountable-owner and legal review. Not approved for publication. The route remains `noindex` and visibly identifies unresolved fields.

**Version:** Draft 0.1, 24 August 2026

**Storage direction:** Separate Supabase marketing database

The visitor-facing draft in `app/privacy/page.tsx` covers controller accountability; data categories, purposes and proposed lawful bases; processors and transfers; retention criteria; individual rights; cookies; complaints; and versioning.

## Required before publication

- Registered legal entity, registered address and company number
- Monitored privacy contact and accountable privacy owner
- Confirmation whether Widian has or requires a data protection officer
- Approved lawful bases and legitimate-interests assessment
- Exact Supabase and Vercel regions, contracts, subprocessors and transfer safeguards
- Retention/review periods by processing purpose
- Calendar, email, analytics and security-monitoring providers
- Cookie scan results and consent configuration

Compliance is expressed as an operating commitment supported by controls, not as a certification or guarantee. An EU region is not described as eliminating international transfers because subprocessors, support or remote access may still create a restricted transfer.

Review sources: ICO right-to-be-informed, international-transfer, and cookies/PECR guidance.
