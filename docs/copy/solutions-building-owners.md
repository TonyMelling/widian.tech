## 1. Page objective

Give portfolio-level owners and responsible persons confidence they can see the current truth across all their buildings, not just what's been reported to them.

## 2. Primary audience

Managing agents, responsible persons, building owners, property/asset-management directors, housing providers.

## 3. Visitor question

Can I establish the current truth across my buildings?

## 4. Primary headline

> **See what's true across your portfolio — not just what's been reported.**

*(Proposed — needs Brand Strategist + Senior Copywriter approval.)*

## 5. Short standfirst

> Every building in your portfolio keeps its own record. Nothing is true because a report said so once — it's true because the record still says so today.

## 6. Narrative sequence

Story per `SPEC.md` §7.4: portfolio truth, open risk/action visibility, accountable delegation, evidence of management activity, verified closure history.

- **Portfolio truth:** See open findings and outstanding actions across every building you're responsible for, not building-by-building.
- **Open risk and action visibility:** Know what's overdue and what's blocking closure without chasing a spreadsheet.
- **Accountable delegation:** When you delegate an action, the record shows who it went to and what they've done with it. *[Claim: Multi-organisation roles and permissions — Verified; Time-stamped attribution and audit history — Verified.]*
- **Evidence of management activity:** Your own oversight — reviews, delegations, decisions — is part of the record, not a separate audit trail you have to assemble after the fact.
- **Closure history:** For your highest-priority findings, closure carries your organisation's own acceptance decision, not just a contractor's word. *[Claim: Client verification and closure — Qualified, P1/P2 only. Do not claim every closure is client-verified.]*

## 8. Objections answered

- *"How is this different from a compliance dashboard?"* → the record persists and connects across buildings; it isn't a report aggregator.
- *"Do I have to verify every single finding myself?"* → no — only priority (P1/P2) findings require your organisation's acceptance; see 6.
