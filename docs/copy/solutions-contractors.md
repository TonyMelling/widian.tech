## 1. Page objective

Reassure contractors that they'll get a clear scope, know exactly what evidence is required, and understand that submitting evidence and having it accepted are two different, separate moments.

## 2. Primary audience

Remedial contractors, including those without an existing Widian account.

## 3. Visitor question

Will I receive a clear scope and know what evidence is required?

## 4. Primary headline

> **Know your scope. Submit your evidence. Know what happens next.**

*(Proposed — needs Brand Strategist + Senior Copywriter approval.)*

## 5. Short standfirst

> You don't need an account to complete a job through Widian. You need a link, a clear scope, and a way to show your work is done.

## 6. Narrative sequence

Story per `SPEC.md` §7.4: exact location and scope, required evidence, straightforward guest workflow, cost and completion information, clear separation between submission and acceptance.

- **Exact location and scope:** Work orders reference the specific element and finding — not a general instruction.
- **Required evidence:** The evidence needed to close out the job is defined upfront, not discovered after the fact.
- **Guest workflow:** Complete a work order through a secure link, without needing a full account first. *[Claim: Guest contractor completion — Verified.]*
- **Clear separation between submission and acceptance:** Submitting your evidence isn't the same as the job being accepted — you'll know which stage you're at. *[Claim: Independent inspection — Qualified; Client verification and closure — Qualified, P1/P2 only. Frame honestly: independent review confirms the work; client acceptance applies only to priority findings.]*

## 7. Proof points

| Proof point | Claim area | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Secure guest-link completion | Guest contractor completion | Verified |
| Evidence submission distinct from independent/client acceptance | Independent inspection; Client verification and closure | Qualified |

## 8. Objections answered

- *"Do I need to create an account just to do one job?"* → 6 — no, the guest workflow is built for exactly this.
- *"How do I know if my work has actually been accepted?"* → the submission/acceptance distinction directly answers this, honestly scoped to what's actually enforced today.
