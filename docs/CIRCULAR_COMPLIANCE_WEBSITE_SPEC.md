# Circular Compliance Website Specification

**Status:** Strategic website specification; not authority to claim unbuilt functionality

## Objective

Evolve the Widian website from a strong Building Record story into a joined-up expression of Widian's continuous compliance model without losing the approved passive-fire wedge or verified-closure discipline.

## Narrative Architecture

The website should explain three nested ideas in this order:

1. **Building Record** — the persistent object.
2. **Verified closure** — the differentiating case outcome.
3. **Circular Compliance** — the continuing lifecycle around the record.

Do not lead with Circular Compliance in a way that makes visitors learn new jargon before understanding the problem Widian solves.

## Lifecycle

`Know → Inspect → Act → Verify → Prove → Monitor → repeat`

Website storytelling may visualise this lifecycle, but each stage must map to either a supportable current capability or a visibly labelled future state.

### Know
Establish current building/element state and outstanding uncertainty.

### Inspect
Capture structured observations and evidence.

### Act
Assign and progress accountable remediation.

### Verify
Distinguish completion, independent checking and client verification.

### Prove
Show the evidence trail and issued records Widian can substantiate.

### Monitor
Explain recurrence, future inspection and continuing attention only to the extent supported; future monitoring functionality must be labelled until built.

## Widian Thread

The Widian Thread may become the website's principal narrative motion device. It should connect real concepts: building identity, inspection, finding, responsibility, evidence and verification.

Requirements:

- never use motion as the only way to understand the lifecycle;
- provide reduced-motion behaviour;
- avoid decorative constant Ember lines;
- use gaps/interruption intentionally to communicate unresolved responsibility;
- reserve verified green for genuinely verified states;
- keep mobile performance and comprehension ahead of spectacle.

## Physical/Digital Story

Future building plaques, QR identifiers and element tags may be shown as **vision/future ecosystem** material only until a dedicated product implementation is approved and shipped.

Do not imply that scanning a public QR currently exposes a live Building Record unless the application has a deliberately designed, security-reviewed projection.

## Page Implications

### Home
Keep the core proposition dominant. Introduce Circular Compliance after the visitor understands the Building Record and closure problem.

### Platform/Product
Explain the persistent record and lifecycle. Current capability must be distinguishable from roadmap vision.

### How it works
Use the lifecycle to explain operational movement, while retaining the more granular canonical status model where detail is needed.

### Evidence/Trust
Explain provenance, verification and claims carefully; do not turn cryptographic implementation details into marketing theatre.

### Future/vision content
May introduce the connected physical/digital ecosystem, but must be labelled and separated from current product proof.

## SEO

Do not sacrifice established search intent around fire safety, passive fire, fire doors, fire stopping, remediation and building compliance merely to optimise for the coined phrase "Circular Compliance". Treat the new phrase initially as an ownable brand/category concept supported by conventional search language.

## Conversion

Circular Compliance should strengthen the buyer's reason to act: Widian is not another place to file reports; it maintains continuity from truth to action to evidence and onward monitoring.

Do not use unsupported ROI, risk-reduction percentages or compliance guarantees.

## Acceptance Criteria

A website implementation is acceptable when:

- the Building Record remains understandable without prior knowledge of Circular Compliance;
- verified closure remains clearly differentiated from contractor completion;
- current and future capabilities are visibly distinguishable;
- the Widian Thread improves comprehension rather than decoration;
- mobile/reduced-motion/accessibility requirements pass;
- every changed product/security claim is reviewed against the Claims Register;
- cross-repository product impact has been checked.
